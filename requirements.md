# Requirements

This document outlines the functional and non-functional requirements for the Patient Health Care System (PHCS), a comprehensive solution designed to automate patient onboarding, facilitate collaboration among healthcare partners, and support patient registration, consent capture, and lifecycle tracking.

## Functional Requirements

- Patient registration and enrollment management
- Patient consent management
- Consultation and appointment scheduling
- Integration with internal healthcare systems and master data repositories
- Integration with external clinical partners
- Reporting and analytics

## Non-Functional Requirements

- Scalability: The system should be capable of handling a minimum of 5,000 patient onboarding transactions per hour, with scalability to support higher volumes as required.
- Availability: The system should have a high availability of 99.99% to ensure seamless collaboration and data accessibility.
- Security: The system should implement robust security measures to protect patient and program-related information, maintain regulatory compliance, and provide a secure collaboration platform for healthcare providers and partners.
- Performance: The system should have a response time (latency) for critical operations of under 2 seconds to provide a seamless user experience.
- Reliability: The system should have a mean time between failures (MTBF) of over 1,000 hours to ensure data integrity and system availability.
- Compliance: The system should comply with relevant regulations and standards, such as HIPAA, GDPR, and CCPA.
