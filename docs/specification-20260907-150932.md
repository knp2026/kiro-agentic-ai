Project Specification

AI-Driven Banking Contract Retrieval with Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)

1. Project Overview

The objective of this project is to provide bank customers with a secure and efficient way to access information related to their contracts through a conversational service channel.

Customers shall be able to request contract information using natural language and receive concise, understandable responses. The solution shall ensure that only authorized customers can access their own information. Sensitive requests (e.g., requests involving personal data or significant financial transactions) can be reviewed by a human representative if the system's confidence level is below a certain threshold or the request's complexity requires human intervention.

The project aims to improve customer experience, reduce handling times for standard inquiries, and maintain high standards of security, compliance, and auditability.

2. Scope

The solution shall support:

Customer identification and verification before contract information is provided.

Retrieval of customer-specific contract information.

Generation of understandable summaries of contract content.

Continuous customer interaction without requiring repeated authentication during the same session.

Escalation of complex or disputed requests to a human representative.

Recording of relevant activities for auditing and compliance purposes.

The solution shall not support contract modifications, financial transactions, or legally binding decisions.

3. Business Requirements

The solution shall:

Allow customers to inquire about their contracts through a conversational interface.

Ensure that contract information is only accessible to authenticated customers.

Present contract information in a clear and customer-friendly manner.

Reduce the workload of customer service teams by handling standard requests automatically.

Transfer complex or sensitive cases to a human representative when necessary.

Maintain a complete audit trail of customer interactions and decisions.

Protect personal and confidential customer data at all times.

Comply with applicable banking, privacy, and security requirements.

4. Functional Requirements

Authentication

Customers must be verified before contract information is disclosed.

Access must be denied if verification cannot be completed successfully.

Authentication status must remain valid throughout the active customer session.

Contract Information Access

Customers shall only receive information related to their own contracts.

The solution shall be able to locate and provide relevant contract information.

If requested information cannot be verified, the customer shall be informed accordingly.

Customer Communication

Responses shall be clear, concise, and understandable for non-technical users.

Customers shall be able to ask follow-up questions within the same interaction.

The solution shall maintain conversation context throughout the session.

Human Review and Escalation

Requests that are ambiguous, disputed, sensitive, or cannot be answered with sufficient confidence shall be escalated to a human representative.

Escalated cases shall include all relevant interaction context.

Customers shall not be required to repeat information already provided during the session.

5. Non-Functional Requirements

Category

Requirement

Security

Customer information must be protected from unauthorized access.

Privacy

Personal data shall be handled according to applicable privacy regulations.

Availability

The service shall be available during agreed business hours and service windows.

Performance

Customers should receive responses without noticeable delays.

Auditability

All customer interactions and decisions shall be traceable.

Reliability

Service failures shall be handled gracefully without exposing sensitive information.

Usability

Responses shall be understandable and suitable for customer use.



6. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

Customers can securely access information about their contracts.

Only authorized customers can view contract-related information.

Standard customer inquiries are handled automatically.

Complex or sensitive requests are successfully transferred to human representatives.

Customer information remains protected throughout the process.

Regulatory, security, and audit requirements are satisfied.

Customer service effort and response times are reduced while maintaining service quality.

Expected Business Outcome: Improved customer experience, faster access to contract information, increased operational efficiency, and secure handling of customer data while maintaining appropriate human oversight.
